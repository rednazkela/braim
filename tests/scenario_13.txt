SCENARIO 13

BEFORE YOU BEGIN
  - Working directory: provided by operator (cd into it before any command).
  - Mandatory first step: run `braim --help` and read its output completely.
    braim's behavior is defined by that help text; do not rely on prior
    assumptions about how the tool works.

Survey the graph and identify any entries that are not yet integrated with
the rest of the structure — entries that nothing else points to and that
point to nothing else.

For each unintegrated entry, decide what to do: leave it as is for now,
connect it to an existing entry through an additional link, or remove it if
it turned out to be a mistake. Record your decision for each one as part of
the graph itself.

Reply with the list of unintegrated entries, the decision you made for each,
and the IDs of any new connections or status entries you created.
