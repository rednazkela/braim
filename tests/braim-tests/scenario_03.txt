Several of the multi-word concepts in this domain are not symmetric: one of the
parts carries more of the concept's identity than the others. For example, a
"Library Card" is foremost a card, with the library being the issuer; a
"Reading Room" is foremost a room, with reading being the activity it
accommodates.

For each compound concept that combines two or more simpler concepts, set the
internal weights so they reflect which part is more central to that concept's
identity in this domain. Briefly note your reasoning for each adjustment.

Reply with the list of concept IDs you adjusted and the weights you chose.
