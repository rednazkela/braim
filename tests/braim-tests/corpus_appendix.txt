APPENDIX: DIGITAL SERVICES AT THE RIVERSIDE PUBLIC LIBRARY

In 2003 the library launched a Website where members search the catalog from
home. A search result shows whether a book is on the shelf or out on loan.
Members sign in to a Patron Account using the account number printed on their
Library Card.

Through the Patron Account, a member may place a Hold Request on any book
currently out on loan. When the book returns, the front desk sets it aside for
three days. A member may also request one Renewal per loan, extending the loan
by an additional three weeks, unless another member holds a Hold Request on
the same book.

The library added a Digital Loan service in 2004. A Digital Loan delivers an
E Book or an Audiobook to the member's device for the standard three-week
period. Digital Loans require no physical return; the file expires on its own.
The annual report notes that Digital Loans accounted for one in five loans by
2006, and that the Website cost eleven thousand dollars to build, funded from
the library's operating budget without a new bond.
