  - Working directory: provided by operator (cd into it before any command).

A resident filed a complaint: they can no longer check out books from the
library. You have been asked to work backwards through the reason this
happened and leave a record that a later reader can replay from the
complaint down to the underlying rule that ultimately explains it.

Capture each step of the explanation as its own entry, grounded in the
library's documented policies, and connect each step to the one that
explains it, so the entries form a single trace from the surface complaint
to the bottom rule. The chain the corpus supports is roughly: the borrowing
block, the unpaid balance that triggers a block, the overdue returns that
ran up that balance, and the loan-period rule underneath it all.

When you are done, have the tool replay the whole trace starting from the
complaint entry.

Reply with: the entries you created (IDs and text), each connection you
recorded and the command you used, and the tool's replayed trace including
which entry it reports as the bottom of the chain.
